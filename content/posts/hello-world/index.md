I made some attempts at writing before. Many, actually. It would usually not be consistent because
I'd get distracted. I'd not have anything interesting to write about (or, rather, think that no one
would read what I have to say). My then-undiagnosed iPad baby brain syndrome would get in the
way. I'd feel guilty for not keeping up, then abandon the whole ordeal.

But I did not actually stop writing. I'd write long reddit comments on niche topics. Or rant to
people who'd listen to me. It's just that I did not actually have anything to look back to. Then,
the universe started sending me signs. [Advice for a friend who wants to start
a blog](https://www.henrikkarlsson.xyz/p/start-a-blog). [Writing against the global
lobotomy](https://kerkour.com/writing-against-the-global-lobotomy). Moses had his burning bush and
I had people I looked up to and respected appearing to me in my feeds telling me to
<mark>WRITE</mark>.

When I gave myself the goal to write more, I decided on a nice, achievable goal of 6 or so posts
over the next year. And so I started setting things up. But the more I set things up, the more
things I realised I should have on here. Like, I want to have [slash
pages](https://slashpages.net/). I want to have [preview
images](https://ognjen.io/automatic-jekyll-post-previews/).
[Microformats](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTML/Guides/Microformats). [Indie
web](https://indieweb.org/).

And I didn't want to appear as a single-dimensioned nerd (not that there's anything wrong with
that). If this place was going to be about me, then I wanted it to be about the whole me. I wanted
to move my (sparse) music reviews to this place. I realised I do not have a consistent place to put
my photos that isn't Instagram[^1]. I could put recipes for all the pastries I've made recently. And
put up my proud portfolio of plants. And all the cool web features I could add, like native shares,
animations, syndication, feeds... So many things! Did I mention the iPad baby brain syndrome
already? Yeah, at some point this entire blog had a skeumorphic design that looked like it was
printed out on perforated dot-matrix paper, with business cards and a (very poorly drawn) printer
used for navigation, all rendered with SVGs so that it scaled and could be animated and transition
between dark and light modes. Probably didn't need that.

Anyway, the more things I thought of, the less I did, and the more guilty I felt looking at the goal
that remained unfulfilled. So, I decided to say "fuck it", trimmed off everything that wasn't ready,
and shipped it. And I'll build it live. It's for me, anyway, and if I am a work in progress, this
place can be as well.

It's true that I might get only a handful of views over the lifetime of this website. But, if
I don't write anything, I only guarantee to myself that no one will read what I write. So, I'll give
it another shot. Also, dextroamphetamine helps I think.

[^1]: This actually led me to a big rabbit hole on encoding HDR images which will eventually be
    a topic of a another post.
